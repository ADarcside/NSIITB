## Before (5 min)

- [ ] Owner consent. Reference: ______________________
- [ ] Room clear of people and pets. Doors and cupboards closed. Fans off.
- [ ] All lights on. Windows closed (curtains still). Direct sun covered. Mirrors covered, screens off.
- [ ] Tape A–B = **2.000 m** along the walk line. Tape C–D = **1.000 m** across it. Labelled. Photo of the tape at each pair.
- [ ] Phone 100 % charge (or power bank), ≥ 25 GB free, Do Not Disturb on.
- [ ] Video **4K, 30 fps, 1x**, no zoom. Action / Cinematic / Night off. Landscape.
- [ ] **AE/AF Lock** on a mid-grey wall patch.

## Filming (one video, do not cut, 30 to 35 min, full plan)

Start time: ________   (finished under 30 min = too fast)

- [ ] 1. Start pan on mark A: 2 turns (level, down 30°)
- [ ] 2. End to end along the aisle, 4 to 6 m: 4 lengths each way (chest level / chest up 30° / waist down 30° / high down 20°)
- [ ] 3. Side walls and bunks from across the aisle: 4 laps (chest level / high up 30° / waist down 40° / low level)
- [ ] 4. Every corner from 1.5 m: 4 sweeps (level / up / down / low)
- [ ] 5. **Ceiling**, phone high: 3 lines (straight up / 45° left wall top / 45° right wall top)
- [ ] 6. Floor: 2 lines, waist, aimed **1 m ahead** (feet and shadow out of shot)
- [ ] 7. Each lower bunk at 1.0 to 1.5 m: chest / low looking up, then slide along it (covers the top bunk's underside)
- [ ] 7b. Each **top bunk** from the aisle, phone at full reach: 3 lengths (mattress-edge level / 30° down / 30° down a step back) + 60° arcs at both ends. Ladder 2–3 rungs optional. Never stand on bunks.
- [ ] 8. Under bunks and desks at 0.5 to 1.0 m: 2 passes, low
- [ ] 9. Desks, cupboards, shelves at 1.0 m: 3 heights
- [ ] 10. Door, window, door wall at 0.7 to 1.0 m: 2 arcs each
- [ ] 10b. Trouble spots from 1–2 m, facing them, 3 slow passes each: high corner above cupboards ☐ AC vent ☐ curtain ☐ ________
- [ ] 11. Tape marks from 2 angles
- [ ] 12. End pan on mark A: 2 turns

Walk while you film (never just turn). 1 step every 1.5 s. Each surface 4 times. Rest by pausing, never by stopping the recording. Never closer than 0.5 m. Never film your own feet or shadow; light behind the phone. Both hands, 1x, never zoom. Moved objects at (time): ______________
